Experience sights like never before

MountAR began its journey in the picturesque town of Dhulikhel, Nepal, a place renowned for its breathtaking mountain views and rich cultural heritage. Inspired by the idea of enhancing the experience for visitors, the concept of blending technology with exploration was born.

Scan QR Codes at View Towers

MountAR brings augmented reality to your fingertips. Simply scan the QR code at designated view towers to unlock detailed AR content about the landmarks around you.

Explore Landmarks in 360° AR

Once you’ve scanned the code, climb the tower and use your device to see nearby landmarks come alive in 360° augmented reality.

Offline Mode for Exploration

MountAR works even without an internet connection. Whether you’re in a remote area or don’t have access to Wi-Fi, you can still enjoy the full experience.
